3-Stage Progressive Scan Pipeline

Rather than flooding analysts with noisy raw dumps, KeryxOSINT processes targets through three asynchronous Celery worker stages with a 15% minimum confidence threshold and dynamic re-scoring:

Stage 01 • Rapid Discovery

SCAN

High-concurrency enumeration across username, email, phone, and domain vectors with automatic HTTP 403/429 rate-limit isolation.

  • Queries 750+ social, developer, forum, and gaming platforms
  • Checks 340+ email registration endpoints without alerting targets
  • Filters out false positives and rate-limited noise before display
Stage 02 • Visual & Bio Forensics

DEEP SCAN

Extracts profile metadata, downloads avatars locally, and dynamically re-scores identity confidence across discovered accounts.

  • Computes 64-bit dHash perceptual hashes to match reused avatars
  • Parses ABOUT pages and bios for secondary handles, emails, and links
  • Elevates or penalizes match scores based on cross-platform consistency
Stage 03 • Recursive Pivoting

DARK SCAN

Launches automated secondary sweeps from newly uncovered selectors and correlates deeper forensic artifacts.

  • Nested keyword and selector pivoting from harvested bios and captions
  • Git repository commit author email extraction and metadata forensics
  • Archived snapshot verification and cryptographic SHA-256 vault sealing

Frequently Asked Questions

What is KeryxOSINT and who developed it?

KeryxOSINT is a local-first open-source intelligence and digital forensics workstation created by Kalakkalx at Hollowboat. It correlates usernames, emails, phone numbers, domains, Git commits, and public social media artifacts on your local machine without sending queries to third-party SaaS servers.

How does KeryxOSINT prevent false positives across common usernames?

Unlike basic username scrapers that treat every HTTP 200 response as the same person, KeryxOSINT caches profile avatars locally, calculates 64-bit dHash visual similarity scores, cross-checks bio metadata, and isolates conflicting accounts until corroborating evidence is found.

What export formats does the local evidence vault support?

Every completed investigation is stored in a local SQLite database with cryptographic SHA-256 hashes and can be exported immediately to structured JSON, CSV, or print-ready PDF forensic reports.
